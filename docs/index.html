How it plays

Every house is an airflow puzzle.

You get a floor plan, an air handler and a metal budget. The air goes wherever your ducts send it, and every room you leave stuffy costs you money.

Run the ductsDrag duct from the air handler toward the rooms. Each tile costs money and metal.
Split the flowTees and branches send air into more rooms. Smoother fittings waste less of it.
Aim the registersPoint registers and diffusers across each room. Cap every open end so nothing leaks into the attic.
Earn and expandCoverage pays every second. Buy upgrades, then move on to bigger houses.

The parts

Cheap and leaky, or smooth and pricey.

Every part has its own airflow loss, shown as its efficiency rating. Newer parts unlock as you take on bigger jobs.

Inside the game

Real numbers behind every breeze.

A live airflow modelFan pressure, friction and fitting losses decide how much air every branch gets. Hover any part to see its airflow in CFM.
Room-by-room coverageA heatmap shows where the air actually reaches. Doorways let it drift into the next room; walls stop it cold.
Upgrades that matterA stronger blower, smoother duct liners, better contracts and more metal per job.
Plays nice on a MacSharp on Retina, autosaves as you go, and has undo, copy and paste, and a dark mode.

Get started

Installed in a minute.

1. Install on Mac
  1. Open Airflow.dmg and drag Airflow into Applications.
  2. Open Airflow. If macOS says it “could not verify” the app, click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Airflow.
  4. Enter your Mac password and click Open Anyway again. You only need to do this once.
1. Install on Windows
  1. Download Airflow-Windows.exe. It's the whole game, with nothing to install. Put it anywhere you like, such as your Desktop.
  2. Double-click it. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. You only need to do this once per version.
  3. The first start takes a few seconds while it unpacks.
Updates and your save

Airflow checks for new versions a few seconds after it opens and every few hours. When one is out, the home screen tells you. On Mac, Download Update fetches it, a banner confirms the download, and the new version starts the next time you restart Airflow. Updates are signed, and Airflow refuses any that aren't. On Windows, the button brings you back to this page for the new .exe. Your progress stays on your computer and carries over between versions.

2. Build your first system
  1. Pick Galvanized Duct from the bar at the bottom and drag from the AHU into a room.
  2. Press 4 for transitions and put a Register Boot on the end of the run. Press T to aim it.
  3. Use T-Branches to reach the other rooms, and cap any open end.
